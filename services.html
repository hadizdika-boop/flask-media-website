Services

Services built to improve how your advertising performs.

Flask Media helps businesses run smarter paid media with strategy, optimization, tracking, and reporting designed around measurable growth.

Paid Media Management

We manage paid advertising across the platforms most relevant to your business. This includes campaign setup, audience building, budget management, optimization, performance reviews, and scaling decisions.

  • Meta Ads
  • Google Ads
  • TikTok Ads
  • Snapchat Ads
  • LinkedIn Ads
2

Campaign Strategy

Every successful campaign starts with the right structure. We help define the audience, offer direction, funnel logic, platform mix, budget allocation, and campaign objectives needed to create a stronger performance system.

3

Tracking & Analytics

Paid media is only as strong as the measurement behind it. We support the setup of tracking systems that help you understand what users are doing and where your results are really coming from.

  • GA4
  • Google Tag Manager
  • Meta Pixel
  • Google Ads conversion tracking
  • Event planning
  • Basic reporting setup
4

Optimization & Scaling

We don’t just launch campaigns and leave them running. We test creatives, refine targeting, improve budget allocation, monitor results, and identify where scale is possible.

5

Reporting

You’ll receive clear reporting focused on the metrics that matter most to your business — including spend, leads, purchases, ROAS, CPA, conversion rate, and next-step recommendations.

Optional Creative Production

Creative production is available as an optional add-on for brands that need support with ad creatives, visual assets, campaign statics, and short-form performance-driven content.
